About me

How I like to work.

I'm naturally curious about why things don't work.

The part of product I enjoy most is getting underneath a problem. Why aren't people converting? Why are they contacting support? Why did something we expected to work… not work?

I'll talk to customers, look at the data, figure out what's getting in the way, and build from there. I care about the details, but I also like getting things shipped. And I like working with people who take ownership and care about what we're building together.

Before Cornell, I spent four years building consumer fintech products in India, while the company I worked at grew from an early-stage startup to a billion-dollar business. I got to work closely with the leadership team and see what changes as a company grows that quickly.

Now at Cornell, I'm exploring a much broader set of products and problems.
