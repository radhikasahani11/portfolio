Selected Work

From customer problems to shipped products.

Raise Financial Services

Raise Financial Services is India's leading fintech platform, valued at $1.2 Bn, that provides a variety of investment solutions through its products Dhan and Upsurge. Raise is backed by marquee investors BEENEXT, Mirae Asset, 3one4 Capital, and Rocketship.vc.

Problem

When stock markets fall, users wanted to add more money to their existing mutual fund portfolio, but that required them to do so fund by fund, making it a repetitive process each time. We also found that new investors were taking almost 7 days after onboarding to make their first investment.

Solution

Built a single-click Top-Up feature, so investors could add money across their existing portfolio in a single flow. We also brought relevant fund recommendations into the post-onboarding journey, helping new investors make their first investment sooner and then invest instantly with Top-Up.

The impact
↑$1.5M → $70M+AUM in 10 months
→7 days → <24 hrsTime to first investment
Problem

Every quarter, regulation required unused trading balances to be returned to customers' bank accounts. When customers came back to trade, many assumed the money was still available and only found out otherwise when the trade failed. In trading, even a short delay can mean missing the price or the opportunity.

Solution

Built Auto Fund Transfer to automatically move the required money from a linked bank account after every quarter, so customers could place their trades without having to add funds manually.

The impact
↓12%Trade failures
↑5%Trade volume

Problem

"Where's my money?" was one of the most common questions coming into support. Customers couldn't clearly see when their withdrawal would arrive. At the same time, requests on the backend were being processed one after another, adding unnecessary wait time before the withdrawal process even began.

Solution

Redesigned the withdrawal experience to clearly show status and expected timelines. We also changed backend processing so requests could be handled in parallel, reaching the processing stage faster.

The impact
↓40%Support queries
↑8%Retention

About me
